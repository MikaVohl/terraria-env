# v2 — bounded combat and exploration

Historical v2 contract and evidence. Current v3 tree harvesting, performance and
learning results are in [ITERATION_2026_10.md](ITERATION_2026_10.md). Old checkpoint
evaluation requires the matching preserved v2 package/library; the current
checkout intentionally rejects it.

## Outcome and scope

This milestone replaces the artificial tool-gated ore ladder with a small,
branching early-game task. Combat makes weapons and armor useful; chests make
exploration useful; rope, platforms and Cloud in a Bottle make mobility useful;
healing, campfires and Life Crystals make survival useful. Ordinary ores are
accessible with the starter copper pickaxe. Upgrades improve mining speed.

It is a Terraria-inspired benchmark, **not a faithful Terraria port**. The
universe is fixed: 31 inventory types, 19 recipes, two mob types, 32 achievements.
World geography and loot placement vary by seed. The current baseline still
needs substantial learning work; the first policies do not saturate the task.

The engine, bindings, human UI, replay, correctness tests, throughput probes,
first six PPO learning curves, and a separate Craftax calibration are implemented.
An LLM API evaluator is not implemented yet. `terraria_lite.rules` exports
recipes/action names/achievement names/item IDs directly from the C tables as
the starting player-knowledge interface.

## Environment contract

- Version: **2**; Gym ID: `TerrariaLite-v2`.
- World: 128×96 cells; player two cells tall; default budget 3000 actions.
- Actions: **53**, including ten directional mining and ten placement targets.
- Reward: +1 exactly once per distinct achievement; −1 on death. No health,
  distance, prerequisite, or crafting-progress shaping.
- Evaluation's primary metric: mean distinct achievements within the budget,
  with death rate, episode length, and per-achievement rates alongside it.
- Observation: **2961 float32 values** = 99 cells × 24 terrain categories
  (23 tiles plus unknown), 99 light values, and 486 status values.
- Status includes health/max health, position, remaining-time fraction,
  equipment, cooldowns, selection, inventory, earned achievements, and four
  entity channels per cell: slime presence, bat presence, HP, movement phase.
- Terrain and mobs below the visibility threshold are concealed. Inventory
  uses sqrt(count/999); the former count-20 saturation would conceal recipe
  thresholds such as 30 wood.
- The local observation is partially observable, not a full world map or full
  simulator state. Recurrent policies can learn memory without a supplied planner.
- A done environment returns zero reward on subsequent steps. The native batch
  API does **not** autoreset; the PPO wrapper explicitly records final observations,
  bootstraps time-limit truncations, and resets lanes.
- Checkpoints carry environment version and action/observation dimensions.
  Old v1 policies/tapes and incompatible intermediate v2 schemas are rejected.

### Legal masks, not strategy

Masks depend on physical affordances, recipe requirements, owned equipment, and
cooldowns. They do not choose objectives, prevent spending ingredients on an
unhelpful recipe, insist on upgrades before exploration, or hide dangerous moves.

An attack with no target remains legal. A potion at full HP remains legal and is
consumed. A turn into a wall remains legal when facing changes. Dark-cell mining
and placement permit attempts without leaking hidden terrain through legality.
Noop is always available.

Deliberate abstractions are rules of the game, not secret policy interventions:
pickaxes are permanent tier upgrades, not stackable inventory objects, so
equal/lower-tier pick crafting is rejected by the handler as well as the mask.
Crafting a specific sword or breastplate equips that choice; acquiring a chest
sword does not automatically pick the strongest weapon. Owned swords can be
cycled. Cloud in a Bottle is a passive carried accessory.

## Real Terraria content, simplified simulation

The starter kit is a Copper Pickaxe and Copper Shortsword. Blue Slimes supply
Gel; Cave Bats create underground pressure. Surface supply chests provide a
Wooden Sword and consumables. Underground loot provides Cloud in a Bottle,
rope, torches and potions, with nearby Life Crystals.

Ordinary copper, iron, silver and gold have overlapping depth distributions.
There are no stone pickaxes or fictional lantern upgrades. This subset omits
alternative ore families, prefixes, boss ores, accessories slots, ammo, liquids,
day/night, additional biomes, crafting substitutions, and most Terraria items.

Recipes are shared by simulation, masks, UI and player-knowledge export:

Simulation shortcuts are explicit:

- Melee has a facing-sensitive two-cell arc, wall occlusion, three-tick cooldown
  and one-cell knockback; no continuous collision or projectile simulation.
- Sword damage: copper shortsword 5; wooden 7; copper 9; iron 12; silver 14;
  gold 15. Slime HP/contact damage 25/7; bat 16/12.
- Torso-only armor has defense 0/1/3 (none/wood/iron); no full-set bonuses.
- Contact invulnerability lasts eight ticks. Health starts at 100, caps at 200;
  Life Crystals add 20. Mushroom/Potion recover 15/50, sharing a 60-tick cooldown.
- Natural regeneration starts after 40 safe ticks, then 1 HP per 16 ticks
  (8 near a campfire). These tick constants are benchmark rules, not real-time
  Terraria timings.
- Copper/iron/gold pick mining intervals are 3/2/1 ticks; all ordinary ores
  remain mineable from the start.
- Platforms support one-way landing and deliberate dropping; rope supports
  vertical movement; Cloud in a Bottle provides one additional airborne jump.
- Chests are single-cell one-shot loot containers, not general storage. Furniture
  is compacted to cells; no construction-size or crafting-window simulation.
- Mob motion/spawns use bounded, deterministic grid rules, not Terraria AI.
  At most eight mobs and ten chests exist. Local spawns begin after a grace period;
  far mobs recycle.

These choices retain competing resource uses and progression paths without
claiming that Terraria itself has the same mechanics or difficulty.

## Exact original art and human UI

The pixel frontend loads the local Terraria `ExtractedTextures` directory at
runtime. The default detected installation here is:

```text
/Users/mikavohl/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/Terraria/ExtractedTextures
```

No original textures are committed, redistributed or synthesized. Missing
original sheets fail loading rather than substituting artwork. The renderer
crops and nearest-samples original sheets to the smaller grid.

Inventory Item IDs are centralized in `include/tiles.h`. Picks load original
Item_3509 / Item_1 / Item_3521. Slime uses NPC_1's original base sheet without
inventing a recolor; Cave Bat uses NPC_49's five-frame strip. Body armor comes
from original Armor_32 (wood) and Armor_2 (iron) sheets. Closed/open chest crops,
mushrooms and equipment have separate original assets.

The UI now displays health/max health, equipped pick/sword/armor, cooldowns,
visible enemies with HP bars, a wrapped 31-item inventory with tooltips,
19 recipes with up to three ingredients, and all 32 achievement tooltips.
Recipe affordances use the same core mask. Hovering darkness does not reveal
hidden terrain. The terminal also displays mobs and the expanded HUD.

The logical pixel viewport is 480×416, sidebar 256 pixels, bottom HUD 112 pixels.
Integer zoom is reduced to fit the display. Human idle time advances the living
world; pause explicitly. Focus loss pauses human SDL play and clears held input.

## Correctness and speed gate

Mutable state belongs to each `Env`; no heap allocation, I/O, SDL or Python work
occurs in `env_step`. Mobs/chests occupy fixed arrays. The per-env terrain-light
cache invalidates on terrain/source changes; the player contributes an exact
bounded local light wave. An independent full-map oracle tests equivalence.
Apple dispatch partitions independent lanes; other platforms use the serial
fallback. The Python batch wrapper owns its environments and rejects duplicate
or closed lanes.

Verification completed:

- `make check`: 100-seed determinism, world/body/inventory/health bounds,
  finite observation ranges, observation purity and reference-light parity;
  targeted combat, armor, cooldown, healing, crystal, recipes, mining,
  platform/rope/double-jump/chest and mask tests; unique rewards and done handling.
- AddressSanitizer + UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer: core and same selftest passed.
- `python -m terraria_lite.ppo_selftest`: termination/truncation/GAE checks,
  **64,000 exact scalar/native transitions** including resets across 1/2/4/8
  workers; legal finite policy support and LSTM step/sequence reset equivalence.
- `make check-pixel`: original-asset loading, all three armor variants, wrapped
  inventory and recipe hit tests. Render fixtures are not training trajectories.
- 5120 sampled MPS actions were legal using CPU categorical sampling of GPU
  probabilities. This checks the workaround; it is not a long-run MPS study.
- A 3000-action final MLP tape matched Python and C/SDL replay exactly
  (health 100, achievement mask 881198023, return 13).
- Frozen v1 rebuilt and its historical invariant/expert tests passed.

The provisional acceptance gate was **no more than 2× v1 rollout slowdown**.
Measured here on Apple M5 Pro, 48 GiB, macOS 26.4:

| Probe | Median transitions/sec | Env bytes |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Frozen v1, original lighting, identical single-thread C harness | 27,570 | 25,256 |
| Frozen v1, cached-light prototype, same harness | 31,708 | 25,256 |
| v2 production core, same harness | 54,886 | 38,576 |
| v2 Python native batch, 1 worker, NumPy encoding included | 48,808 | 38,576 |
| v2 Python native batch, 4 workers, NumPy encoding included | 127,088 | 38,576 |

Gate passes. Net v2 engineering performance is about 1.73× the cached v1 probe.
This is **not** an isolated measurement of the causal cost of mobs: worlds, action
sets, cooldowns and death/reset frequencies changed. The core harness includes
mask/step/observe/autoreset, 64 environments, 262,144 transitions × three repeats.
The vector probe uses 65,536 transitions × three repeats. Initial world generation
is outside timing; subsequent resets are inside. Different probes use different
samplers and cannot be read as additive profiling components.

Env state memory grows 52.7% versus original v1, largely because of cached lighting, but remains about
38.6 KB per world. The 64×128×2961 float observation rollout alone is about 97 MB.
Neither simulation memory nor file I/O currently looks like the limiting resource.
The cached v1 prototype has an external static cache omitted from its sizeof(Env)
figure; this is not a total-memory comparison against that prototype.
The vector probe ran on an active desktop (one overlapped a short random-policy
evaluation), not an isolated hardware ceiling. Training includes neural work and
validation; rollout SPS is not PPO training SPS.

Raw measurements and caveats are in [V2_BENCHMARKS.json](V2_BENCHMARKS.json).

```sh
cc -std=c11 -O2 -Iinclude -Isrc tools/core_benchmark.c src/sim.c src/worldgen.c src/light.c src/obs.c -lm -o /tmp/terraria-core-benchmark
/tmp/terraria-core-benchmark
```

## First from-scratch learning curves

Protocol: two general architectures, three independent training seeds each,
**1,048,576 transitions per run**; **6,291,456 total**. Four validations per run,
every 262,144 transitions. CPU training with six Torch threads and four native
simulator workers; 64 environments × 128 steps, four epochs/four minibatches.
MLP has two 512-wide layers; recurrent baseline uses 256 encoder/256 LSTM.
PPO gamma .999, lambda .95, LR .0003 annealed, entropy .01, clip .2.
No cloning, expert initialization, progress shaping, strategic masks, supplied
map/planner, or curriculum.

Training worlds use training_seed × 10,000,000 + episode counter.
Validation: seeds **2,000,001..2,000,128**, stochastic sampler seed 54321,
3000-action limit. Test: **3,000,001..3,000,128**, reserved and not evaluated.
Reported checkpoints are the final fixed-budget models, not the best validation
checkpoint. Validation runs preserve the training RNG stream.

| Architecture / training seed | Final achievements /32 | Death rate | Wall sec including 4 validations |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| MLP / 101 | 12.3594 | 21.88% | 64.05 |
| MLP / 202 | 12.4922 | 38.28% | 63.03 |
| MLP / 303 | 12.5547 | 35.94% | 62.61 |
| LSTM / 101 | 12.1797 | 37.50% | 124.99 |
| LSTM / 202 | 12.8516 | 32.03% | 152.71 |
| LSTM / 303 | 13.2656 | 31.25% | 132.46 |

Across three training seeds: **MLP 12.4688 ± 0.0997**, **LSTM 12.7656 ± 0.5480**
(mean ± sample SD across training seeds, not confidence intervals).
Mean death rates: 32.03% and 33.59%. Uniform legal random on the same validation
worlds scores **5.2266/32**, with 96.09% deaths. These results establish learning
above random, not robust superiority of LSTM or near-saturation. Some runs
overlapped the separate Craftax calibration; wall times are observed end-to-end
desktop timings, not dedicated-device architecture benchmarks.

### Current learning bottleneck

**None of the six final models crafted a workbench in any validation episode.**
Consequently none reached furnace/anvil/smelting/crafted gear/pick upgrades.
Policies learn collecting, placing, combat, opening chests, healing, exploring,
rope climbing and descent. They often collect ordinary ores, but do not turn
resources into a workshop.

Slime defeats reach roughly 78%/82% of worlds (MLP/LSTM), bats 32%/52%;
deep descent roughly 93–94%. Crystal use is very rare, double jumps absent,
and return-to-surface rare. The task's resource accumulation and long-horizon
dependencies remain unsolved. The modest LSTM mean gain costs roughly twice
the observed wall time; three seeds do not establish an algorithmic winner.

The natural next learning experiments are longer unshaped runs, larger PPO
batches/model capacity and a general spatial encoder, with explicit inventory,
craftability and achievement diagnostics. Do not quietly add crafting advice,
expert data or strategic masks to fix this wall. Revisit game balance only as
a clearly versioned environment change. Retain the untouched test split for a
later frozen evaluation. This milestone does not claim every achievement is
reliably achievable in every random seed or a known optimal 32/32 policy.

Artifacts:
`experiments/v2-first-curves/{mlp,lstm}-{101,202,303}/` contains config, curve,
summary and four versioned checkpoints. These bulky local files are ignored.
[V2_RESULTS.json](V2_RESULTS.json) retains compact curves, all per-achievement
rates, software versions and a **post-run** source hash snapshot.
The early `experiments/v2-smoke` policies predate the final 53-action schema
and are not included in these results.


## Craftax Classic calibration

A separate isolated environment ran the unmodified official feedforward PPO
algorithm, wrapping only logging locally with telemetry disabled. Source:
[Craftax_Baselines at 7ce36fa](https://github.com/MichaelTMatthews/Craftax_Baselines/tree/7ce36fa05b84a2c9e758012f1e6da402e1e3a891).
Dependencies are pinned in [tools/craftax-calibration-requirements.txt](../../tools/craftax-calibration-requirements.txt):
Craftax 1.6.1, JAX/JAXlib 0.4.35, Flax 0.8.5, Optax 0.2.3.

Craftax-Classic-Symbolic-v1, seed 101, CPU, 64 environments × 64 rollout steps,
1,048,576 transitions, optimistic resets disabled; other official PPO defaults.
Final training batch: **5.6333/22 achievements**, return 4.7333, episode length
158.93. Actual synchronized wrapper wall: **327.79 sec**.

This is a one-seed pipeline/learning calibration, **not a paper replication,
held-out score, leaderboard comparison or direct Terraria/Craftax difficulty
comparison**. The upstream environment/rewards remain unchanged, including
health-related reward. Cold compilation/setup and concurrent desktop training
are included. The upstream printed timer initially returned before asynchronous
callbacks completed; use the wrapper's `jax.effects_barrier()` completion time,
not that misleading early timer.

Reproduction (separate environment; do not replace the project's dependencies):

```sh
mkdir -p experiments/craftax-calibration/upstream
curl -L https://api.github.com/repos/MichaelTMatthews/Craftax_Baselines/tarball/7ce36fa05b84a2c9e758012f1e6da402e1e3a891 -o /tmp/craftax-baselines.tar.gz
tar -xzf /tmp/craftax-baselines.tar.gz --strip-components=1 -C experiments/craftax-calibration/upstream
uv venv --python 3.12 experiments/craftax-calibration/.venv
uv pip install --python experiments/craftax-calibration/.venv/bin/python -r tools/craftax-calibration-requirements.txt
experiments/craftax-calibration/.venv/bin/python tools/craftax_calibration.py \
  --out experiments/craftax-calibration/run \
  --env_name Craftax-Classic-Symbolic-v1 --num_envs 64 --num_steps 64 \
  --num_minibatches 4 --total_timesteps 1048576 --seed 101 \
  --no-use_optimistic_resets --wandb_project terraria-lite-calibration
```

Local protocol/curve/summary are in `experiments/craftax-calibration/run`.
Do not overwrite an existing calibration when rerunning; choose a fresh `--out`.
