# v10 PPO at 1B on the current default rules, 2026-11-10

Question (DECISIONS.md B1, B8, B12): every earlier v10 PPO number was measured before the support
rules / leaf visuals (`d277b85`) and before explicit tools, tool damage and background walls
(`4ef5afa`). How do the reference arms score on the current default at the standard 1B budget, and
what did the new rules change?

- **Return to surface 0, lens 0, Demon Eye / Zombie kills 0, iron pickaxe 0 in all 15 runs**, at
  every validation point, with and without background walls. Tier 4 comes only from "find a
  Demon Altar". So the B4 failure persists on the new default, and the wall rules (which broke
  scripted pillaring, B13) are not what blocks it at 1B.
- Deaths rose from 0.30-0.31 to 0.51-0.54 of episodes (mean length 7,250-7,280 steps vs 8,207 for
  old generic 3001), with 20-35 night deaths per 128 worlds vs 9 for old generic 3001. Removing the walls takes back about a third of
  that (0.46). The rest comes from the other new rules (explicit tools, tool damage, support
  rules); this study does not separate them.
- The anvil rate rose (generic 15% -> 38%, shaped 35% -> 45%); furnace and altar rates are
  unchanged. Policies spend a little more time near the surface and less in the caverns than the
  old generic policy (0.75 vs 0.80 of steps in the caverns) and reach slightly shallower peaks.
- Without walls the shaped arm is better on almost every count: tier 3 41% vs 33%, anvil 55% vs
  45%, altar 29% vs 22%, deaths 0.46 vs 0.54, and more time in the caverns.

## Cost

15 runs, 18.1 run-hours of wall time at 2 runs per GPU (about 9.0 GPU-hours; the main 10
runs 11.7 run-hours, about 5.8 GPU-hours), 2 h 13 min elapsed, plus CPU diagnostics. Training
throughput: generic 299k, shaped 227k, no walls 235k steps/s (2 runs sharing a GPU), the same as
the previous generic run (299k).

## Conclusion for the freeze

- The B8/B12 re-run is done: on the current default, generic PPO 1B scores IQM 33.9% [32.7, 35.0]
  and shaped 34.3% [33.4, 35.0], inside the 25-35% target band. The new rules do not invalidate
  the v10 release choice (B1).
- Two caveats for the reference agents (C4): the shaped arm's margin over generic is now within
  noise (0.4 pp), and deaths are much higher than before (about half of all episodes end in
  death). Return to surface, lens and iron pickaxe stay at 0, so B4 remains open on the new
  default.
- Background walls cost the shaped arm 3.6 pp [1.9, 4.9] (5/5 seeds) and push its score from
  above the band (37.8%) back into it. That is a difficulty effect of a fidelity rule, not a bug,
  but if B12 is revisited, this number is the price of the walls.
