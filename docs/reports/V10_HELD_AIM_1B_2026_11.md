# v10 PPO 1B with held place and aimed attacks, 2026-11-11

Question (DECISIONS B14, B15): `HELD_PLACE` (held standalone place) and `AIM_ATTACK` (8-direction
attacks) are default on since `2314765`. How do the reference PPO arms do on them at the standard
1B budget, compared with the current-default baseline (`V10_PPO_1B_2026_11.md`)?

Short answer: the first version of held place masked a place on stock alone, and PPO turned that
into place spam (40% of steps, 87% of them failing) and scored lower. After the mask was tightened
(`4ede178`), place use and failure rates are back at the baseline's level. The two finished seeds
per arm score within about 1 pp of the paired baseline (generic -0.71, shaped -0.86 pp, one seed
higher in each arm). Deaths and night deaths are lower. The run had only two seeds per arm, so
these numbers are evidence, not a ranking (below).

## Setup

- Arms `v10_gen_g9999` (generic) and `v10_shaped_g9999` (recipe/boss-chain potential), unchanged
  from the baseline. 1,073,741,824 steps per run, validation every 128M steps and at the end on
  the 128 validation worlds 23,000,001-23,000,128. `tools/policy_diagnostics.py` runs on each
  final checkpoint (128 episodes, same worlds, same sampling seed). Baseline: V10PPO1B (`f196c03`
  default, library sha256 `19f13ae4...`), seeds paired.
- **Stock-only mask** (the first version of the held place): library `2314765`,
  `make -C perf/v7 V7=$PWD/variants/v10 BUILD=build-v10 VARIANT="-DINV_LOG=1"`, sha256
  `2f1888cd0be21ac2d4e265727e74b67cc93eab4f9cc4c324302c611916576dc0` (selftest 198/198, fid_test
  106/106, `check_v10.py` passed). eg18 `~/Projects/RL-heldaim/experiments/eg18-v10heldaim-20261111`,
  queue `tools/queue_20261111_v10heldaim.json`.
- **Tightened mask** (current default): library `4ede178`, same build command, sha256
  `f6413078a1684d54b65e5f70f028e59a5ee1e95670d093a03fdee68d1e30e971`. The mask, its tests and its
  cost are described in `HELD_PLACE_AIM_2026_11.md`. eg18 `.../eg18-v10heldtight-20261111`, queue
  `tools/queue_20261111_v10heldtight.json`.
- Trainer: main's trainer (train-speed merged). `perf/v10/fingerprint.sh` on HIP 7 with the
  baseline library gives `10e562162c02ceda`, the documented fingerprint, so the trainer is
  unchanged. The stock-only library gives `bffc4b1ef061b322`, which is expected because the
  environment differs. The baseline itself ran on the older `f196c03` trainer, which has the same
  fingerprint.
- Hardware: eg18, HIP GPU 7 only (= rocm-smi GPU[5] by PCI bus 0x98; HIP 4 = rocm-smi GPU[7]).
  The GPU was idle before the runs. Trainers ran on cores 300-339, diagnostics on 340-383.
  `tools/gpu_queue.py` ran 2 runs per GPU.
- Concurrency probe: shaped runs of 16M steps each on HIP 7 with the stock-only library. With 2
  concurrent runs: 268k and 262k steps/s per run. With 3: 308k / 211k / 210k (the starts were
  staggered). With 4 (simultaneous start): about 184k each. Going from 2 to 3 runs costs each run
  about 20%, because optimisation time rises from 33 s to 51 s per run (the GPU is the
  bottleneck). That is above the 15% limit, so all runs used 2 per GPU.
- Place outcome diagnostic (new in `tools/policy_diagnostics.py`): a place step succeeded iff the
  stock of the item in hand after the step dropped. `do_place` selects that item, and nothing
  else changes a placeable's stock within a place step. Counts at or above the observation clip
  (999) count as "unknown"; there were none. On 4 random-play episodes this agrees with the
  simulator's "placed" message on 2,349 of 2,353 place steps. All 4 disagreements are cases where
  a mob hit overwrote the message after a real placement. Re-running the diagnostic on the 10
  baseline checkpoints with the baseline library reproduces their scores and night deaths exactly;
  the baseline place numbers below come from that re-run.

### Scope and cancellations

- Stock-only mask: the plan was seeds 3001-3005. Once seed 3001 showed the place spam, Main
  stopped the round: `v10ha_*_1B-3002` were killed at 671M (generic) and 537M (shaped), and
  3003-3005 were never launched (`cancelled.json` in the experiment directory).
- Tightened mask: the plan was generic + shaped on seeds 3001-3003, then AIM_ATTACK only (shaped,
  `HELD_PLACE=0` library `9192d30d...`, seeds 3001-3003). The user reduced the release gate to
  four trials (tightened mask, both arms, seeds 3001 and 3002). `v10ht_gen_1B-3003` was killed
  after about 12 minutes (one 128M point, 16.93%) and `v10ht_shaped_1B-3003` after about 6 minutes
  (no checkpoint). The three AIM-only runs were never launched (`cancelled.json`). As a result,
  this report has no AIM-only control and cannot separate the held-place effect from the
  aimed-attack effect.
- Two seeds per arm is too few for a robust IQM or bootstrap CI (with 2 seeds the IQM is the mean).
  The report therefore gives means and per-seed paired differences only.

- **Deaths:** with the tightened mask, deaths drop from 0.57 to 0.51 (generic) and from 0.55 to

| shaped | stock-only | **40.0** | 47,945 / 319,510 | **87.0** | 11.9 | 88.4% |

- **Wasted places (the B14 risk):** with the stock-only mask, about 40% of all steps are place
  actions and 87% of them do nothing, so about 35% of the episode is wasted and fewer blocks are
  placed than in the baseline (48-50k vs 80-86k per seed). The tightened mask brings place use
  (11%) and failure rate (25-28%) back to the baseline's level. The remaining failures have the
  same cause as in the baseline: the mask lets the agent try placing into hidden cells. In random
  play every masked-in failure was next to a hidden cell (`HELD_PLACE_AIM_2026_11.md`).
- **Directional attacks:** the policies attack on 12-15% of steps (baseline 2.7%), spread almost
  evenly over the 8 aims. For example, tightened generic: forward 34.6k, up-forward 33.7k, up 34.4k,
  up-back 35.0k, back 34.8k, down-back 34.8k, down 34.3k, down-forward 32.1k. Since 7 of the 8 aims
  are non-forward, an even spread puts about 87.5% of attacks off forward, which matches the
  measured share. So at 1B the policy has not learned to prefer particular directions; it spends
  more steps attacking, with near-uniform aim. Attack actions are legal whenever no swing is
  running, so the 8 aims carry 8 times the probability mass of the single old `attack`. This
  probably explains much of the higher attack share [INFERENCE].

## Cost

- Throughput (2 runs sharing HIP 7, main trainer): tightened generic 328k and shaped 311k steps/s;
  stock-only 336k / 315k; probe as above. Run wall time is 0.92 h (generic) and 0.97 h (shaped)
  per 1B run, including validation. The baseline, on the older trainer, took 0.98-1.09 h (generic)
  and 1.27-1.36 h (shaped). The tightened mask costs 4.5% of raw simulation throughput on the
  same action stream, but only about 2% of training throughput, because the GPU accounts for half
  of training wall time.
- Run-hours (trainer wall time including validation): tightened 3.78 (4 runs) + 0.31 (cancelled
  3003); stock-only 1.86 (2 runs) + 1.17 (killed 3002); probe about 0.1. Total about 7.2
  run-hours. Elapsed time on GPU 7: 14:46-16:26 (probe + stock-only round) and 16:53-18:58
  (tightened round), about 3.8 h. CPU diagnostics run beside the trainers, are not included, and
  take about 5-10 min per run. Baseline place re-diagnostics: 10 checkpoints on CPU.

## Conclusion

- **B14 (held place):** the stock-only mask is not shippable for RL. PPO spends 40% of its steps
  on failed places and loses 1.2 pp (generic) and 5.0 pp (shaped) on the paired seed. The
  tightened mask (`4ede178`: step-start legality OR legality on the step's predicted no-input body
  path) fixes this. Place use and failure match the baseline, the naive scripted climb now
  succeeds 32/32 in a shaft and in open air, and scores are within about 1 pp of the paired
  baseline on 2 seeds per arm.
- **B15 (aimed attacks):** policies use all 8 aims about equally and attack 5x more often than
  the baseline. Deaths and night deaths are lower than the baseline on the paired seeds, but
  there are no Demon Eye kills or lenses. Without the cancelled AIM-only control, the effects of
  B14 and B15 cannot be separated.
- Return to surface, lens and iron pickaxe stay at 0 (B4 open). Two seeds per arm cannot rule
  out a small score cost of up to about 1 pp. A robust IQM/CI would need seeds 3003-3005.
