## Results and interpretation

Luna reached **a furnace and copper smelting in both longer episodes**, but died
in both. The shared conservative cost guard stopped the third world at129
actions. This is evidence that the current v7 contract permits model-driven
crafting with public recipes, not evidence of reliable mastery or superiority
over PPO. All three planned attempts are included below; no best-of selection.

All three made workbenches; none made an anvil, metal sword, armor or upgraded
pickaxe. Furnace/copper-smelt first-unlock steps were2020/2035 in the first
world and736/741 in the second. The third world was alive with58 health when
the guard stopped it; its low score is **budget-censored**, not a completed
3,000-action failure to progress. Neither longer episode reached the inherent
deadline either: death terminated them earlier.

The raw all-attempt mean is **14.667/32** (scores20,19,5). The emitted three-world
bootstrap interval [5,20] and death fraction2/3 are descriptive only. The
`complete=true` field means all three attempts were recorded under their declared
resource limits, **not** three full-action-budget evaluations. Sequential sharing
of the cost guard gave later worlds less remaining budget, so this mean is not
a fair fixed-budget capability estimate or a public leaderboard score. Do not
drop the third world or substitute new worlds to improve it.

### Cost, tokens and execution

- **Actual provider-reported cost: $0.89574603** across445 completed requests.
- **Conservative accounted cost: $1.984693**, below the shared $2 guard; the
  next request's reservation would not fit. The guard intentionally used higher
  token-price ceilings than realized billing. No missing/ambiguous charges.
-6,990,742 prompt tokens +316,010 completion tokens = **7,306,752 total**.
  Completion includes271,671 reported reasoning tokens; do not add them again.
-4,409 primitive actions;61.54 minutes summed episode wall time. Mean API
  latency8.30s, median7.11s. The game did not advance during model inference.
- Every request explicitly used exact `openai/gpt-6-luna` and
  `reasoning={effort:high,exclude:true}`. All445 returned model IDs matched;
  all445 reported provider OpenAI and finish reason `stop`. A requested high
  setting is a request-contract audit, not observation of private reasoning.
- One malformed structured response consumed one noop; no automatic retries,
  model/provider fallback or infrastructure failure. **1,560 known-illegal
  primitive attempts** (35.4% of actions) still consumed ticks.

Replay reconstruction confirms894 illegal attempts in world10,639 in world11,
and27 in world12. Common cases include mining, jumping, blocked horizontal
movement and rope climbing. World11 alone has170 illegal `climb up` and69
illegal `climb down` attempts. These identify execution/navigation weaknesses;
they do not by themselves distinguish geometric misunderstanding from stale
open-loop batches or other causes. Shorter feedback intervals are a plausible
future controlled comparison, **not an experiment launched here**.

The useful distinction from current scratch PPO is **different failure modes**:
these two longer LLM episodes coordinate a workshop but waste many actions and
die; current spatial PPO reliably mines yet rarely starts crafting. The LLM
has pretrained knowledge, explicit recipes and persistent notes, observes only
between batches, and runs on different public worlds. There is no equal-prior,
equal-compute or matched-world head-to-head result. Sol's earlier256-action
single-world smoke also cannot establish a causal Sol/Luna ranking.

The user changed future testing from Sol to **`openai/gpt-6-luna`**, keeping
high reasoning, to support longer/cheaper experiments. The exact model ID,
reasoning and structured-output support were checked against official
[OpenAI documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-luna)
and the live [OpenRouter catalog](https://openrouter.ai/api/v1/models).
Listed short-context rates at launch were $0.10/M input and $0.50/M output;
cache and long-context tiers are retained in the metadata snapshot.
