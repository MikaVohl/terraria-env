Replays

Every LLM attempt on Terraria-Lite is stored as an action tape: the world seed and the list of primitive actions the model chose. This page feeds each of the 12 audited tapes to the real simulator, compiled to WebAssembly, and checks that the episode it rebuilds ends exactly where the audit says it did.

Does the replay match the audit?

Your browser runs viz/terraria.wasm: the v8 core with default knobs, compiled to WebAssembly. With default knobs v8 is byte-identical to frozen v7, the environment these attempts ran on. The page resets the world from the seed on the tape, applies the recorded actions in order and compares the final state with the audited record in data/llm.json.

This is the same check the audit ran natively. For the Luna batch, the frozen CLI verified hash chains, transitions, rewards, observations and final records without model calls, and each tape independently matched SDL headless replay on steps, health, achievement mask and native return (docs/reports/LLM_LUNA_2026_10_17.md, "Completion audit"). The harness study was replay-audited the same way (docs/reports/LUNA_HARNESS_STUDY_2026_10_17.md).

Illegal attempts are recounted here as actions that the simulator's legal-action mask rejected in the state where they were issued. They still cost a game tick.

About this attempt

All audited attempts

Twelve attempts from three studies on frozen v7 (32 achievements, at most 3,000 actions): one GPT-6.1 Sol smoke test capped at 16 calls, the three-world GPT-6 Luna development batch under a shared $2 guard, and the eight-attempt Luna harness study (four harnesses on two paired worlds, $0.35 guard per attempt). No attempt reached 3,000 actions. Cost is the provider-reported cost.

Watching a trained PPO policy

PPO entries are not action tapes. A policy samples its actions, so a PPO score is verified by re-evaluating the saved checkpoint: evaluation uses categorical sampling with a fixed sampler seed and the legal-action mask, so the same checkpoint on the same 128 worlds reproduces its score exactly (method: verification). The leaderboard marks these entries as checkpoint-verified.

The viz page runs one of those checkpoints live in your browser, with its action probabilities, value estimate and entropy next to the game.
