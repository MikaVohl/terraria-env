Terraria-Lite

A deterministic C reimplementation of early Terraria for reinforcement learning and LLM agents. Both kinds of agent see the same observation, act through the same 83 primitive actions and are scored on the same achievements. A second track runs agents in the real game.

TL;DR

  • The simulator steps 1.29 million times per second on one CPU thread. A PPO run trains at about 287k steps/s on one GPU, so 2 billion steps take about two hours. Real Terraria, headless, manages about 1,000 agent actions per second per process.
  • On v8 (32 achievements), the best PPO configurations reach 28.0/32 on held-out test worlds (IQM of 5 seeds). Iron pickaxes appear only at 4B steps and more; one 6B-step run reaches 29.3/32, with an iron pickaxe in 99 of 128 worlds and a climb back to the surface in 104.
  • An LLM (GPT-6 Luna) reaches a furnace and smelts copper for about $0.40 per episode, but wastes a third of its actions on illegal moves and dies. Across 14,848 evaluated PPO episodes, iron chainmail was crafted once and a gold pickaxe never.
  • The two largest single gains for PPO were making the world fair (enough ore and trees: +0.9 on the test worlds) and a longer discount horizon (up to +0.8 at 2B steps). Wider networks and an LSTM changed scores by less than 0.3.
  • v9 is the release candidate: a layered world, night, a boss (Eye of Cthulhu) and 38 achievements in four weighted tiers, so the benchmark stays far from saturated.

Two tracks, one score

The Terraria-Lite track is the fast simulator: RL trains on billions of steps, LLMs act through a text harness with the RL observation. The official Terraria track runs the same contract on the real game through tModLoader, at about a thousandth of the speed. Both report cost and time next to score.

Does our pipeline reproduce Craftax?

Every Terraria-Lite number is only as good as the training pipeline behind it. We are rerunning the official Craftax baselines on our GPUs against the published scoreboard (percent of maximum reward at 1B steps). The pipeline smoke test has run; the 1B reruns have not.
