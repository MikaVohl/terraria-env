What helped RL

PPO on Terraria-Lite v8 (32 achievements, 3,000-step episodes), changing one thing at a time. Scores are achievements per episode on 128 validation worlds unless marked test; the final configurations were also evaluated once on 128 reserved test worlds.

TL;DR

    Bitter-lesson PPO against maximally good RL

    We keep two arms apart so that a score never hides how much the designer helped.

    • Bitter-lesson PPO (generic). No reward shaping and no game knowledge: the agent sees the extrinsic achievement reward only, plus generic exploration that would work in any environment (a 0.01 novelty bonus and a 1% uniform action mix). Better results have to come from compute, horizon and the environment itself.
    • Maximally good RL (disclosed priors). Anything that helps, as long as it is disclosed: potential-based shaping on the recipe graph (the "frontier" potential), a log-scale inventory observation, and later behaviour cloning from demonstrations. Both arms train on the same fair worlds (ore 3x + trees 2x) with the same action set and are scored on the same validation and test worlds.

    Final configurations

    Interquartile mean (IQM) over 5 seeds with a 95% bootstrap confidence interval, as recommended by rliable. Validation and test are 128 worlds each; each seed plays every world once. The Crafter score is the geometric mean of per-achievement success rates (test worlds). Training cost is GPU-hours per seed times an assumed rate, .

    Probability of improvement

    P(row beats column): the chance that a random seed of the row configuration scores higher than a random seed of the column configuration (validation scores, 5 by 5 seed pairs, ties count half).
