Interface (standard Terraria key bindings):
- A / D walk left / right, Space jumps (hold longer to jump higher), S drops through platforms.
- Left mouse uses the held item at the cursor: swing a weapon, mine with a pickaxe, chop with an axe, place a block.
- Right mouse interacts: opens doors and chests, talks to townspeople, equips armor from the inventory.
- Number keys 1-9 and 0 select hotbar slots 1-10; the hotbar is the row of slots at the top left.
- Escape opens and closes the inventory. While it is open, craftable recipes appear at the lower left;
  clicking a recipe crafts it, and the list depends on the items you carry and the stations near you.
- H drinks a healing potion if you carry one.
Screen geometry: the camera keeps your character at the screen centre (about 512, 512). World tiles are
about 16 pixels wide. Your character is about 2 tiles wide and 3 tiles tall. You can mine, chop or place
within about 4-5 tiles of your character.
